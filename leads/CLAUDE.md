# Rules for the leads folder

- Everyone in `leads.csv` is a made-up practice person. They are not real.
- Never add real people or real email addresses. Every address must end in `@example.com`, a reserved domain that can never receive mail.
- Every row needs a `consent` value. Do not add a row without one.
- Never change the `@example.com` addresses of existing rows.
- Keep exactly these columns: `first_name,last_name,email,group,joined_on,consent`.
- Groups are `learners`, `builders` and `leaders` (8 people each).
