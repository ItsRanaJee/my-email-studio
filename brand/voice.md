# Brand voice: The AI Agent Factory, 2nd edition

Who we write for: people who work in AI and want to build and lead companies run by AI Workers.
What we are marketing: the 2nd edition of *The AI Agent Factory* (Panaversity), starting from "The Five Horizons: From Agent to Autonomous Economy".

## Our voice in three words

1. **Clear**: short sentences, plain English, one idea at a time.
2. **Future-looking**: we talk about where AI work is heading and what to do next.
3. **Roadmap-minded**: we give readers a path (the Five Horizons and the certification and portfolio roadmaps), not hype.

Tone: **motivating and honest**. We encourage readers to act, and we also warn them plainly about real limits (for example, that a credential alone proves little). Warnings are facts from the book, never scare tactics.

## Words we use

- **AI Worker** (always capitalized, our key term)
- horizon / Five Horizons
- roadmap
- build and lead
- forecast / indicative (whenever we mention a future date)
- milestone, portfolio, route (from the certification and portfolio roadmaps)
- "Certifications are milestones, not the product."

## Words we avoid

- **secret** (as in "the secret to...", "secrets of...")
- Also avoid, as a safe default: hype words such as "revolutionary" and "game-changing", and fear lines such as "AI will replace you". (Added by Claude from the interview suggestions. Edit if you disagree.)

## Claims we must never make

- No job guarantees: never promise a job, a promotion, income or any career result.
- Never state future dates as fact. The dates for Horizons 2-4 are forecasts, so always call them forecasts or indicative.
- Never invent a price, a launch date or a buy link. Use a clearly marked placeholder until we have a real one.
- Certifications: only say what the certification and portfolio roadmaps page states (https://agentfactory2.panaversity.org/certification-and-portfolio-roadmaps/). Never promise that a person will pass, and never say a certification guarantees a job or income.
- Never say or imply that Anthropic endorses or is affiliated with Panaversity or this book. The PCxx exams are Panaversity's own.
- Never call a PCxx exam a practice test. It is a credential. The readiness check before an Anthropic exam is PCxx plus the snapshot rehearsal.
- Never hide the access limits: Anthropic exams are currently only for people at organizations in the Claude Partner Network, registered with a company email address.
- Dates and domain tables on the certification page can change. Treat them as "as of the page's date" and re-check before any real send.
